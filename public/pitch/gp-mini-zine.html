What we do

GP Mini-Zine.

One zine per Grand Prix. 13 photos of the weekend from the team, 16 pages, two A4 sheets. Fans print it at home, colour in the cover, scan the code on the back and post it with #ARF1. The download needs a login and unlocks a Team Card.
