Mini-Zine

Every Grand Prix, folded into the fan's pocket. A printable 16-page zine with the team's best photos of the weekend, a cover to colour in and a Team Card for downloading it.

The problem

Fans scroll through a whole race weekend.
They keep almost nothing.

Hundreds of photos per Grand Prix land in feeds and are gone by Monday. Nothing stays on a desk, a fridge or a shelf. Nothing reminds a fan of the weekend once the app is closed.

The gap

An open lane in F1

Fans make F1 zines for themselves. We found no team that gives them official race-weekend photos in a format to print, fold and keep.

The idea

One zine per Grand Prix.

  • 16 pages from two A4 sheets, one cut each, no glue
  • 13 photos of the weekend, selected by the team in SlashLens
  • A line-art cover to colour in, it also looks finished as it is
  • A QR code on the back: share the zine with #ARF1
  • Download after login, a Team Card for every zine
For the fan

Eight steps. Ten minutes.

For the team

Small format. Big loyalty levers.

01

Registrations

The download sits behind the login. Every zine is a reason to create an account.

02

Collection

One Team Card per zine, 24 a season. Collecting brings fans back after every race.

03

Creator content

Coloured covers and finished zines are made to be posted. Real fan content with #ARF1.

04

Time with the brand

Printing, folding, colouring: minutes of attention away from the feed, and an object that stays.

05

Partners in print

Room for a partner page. 40% of Gen Z fans are more likely to buy from F1 partners.

06

Near-zero cost

Fans print at home: no print run, no shipping. The tool builds a zine in minutes.

For us: the workflow

From race Sunday to zine on Monday.

Sunday

Select

The photo team picks about 14 shots of the weekend in SlashLens, e.g. a "Zine" collection per GP.

Monday

Build

The Ktulu tool lays out the zine, renders the colour-in cover and the 300 dpi print PDF.

Monday

Publish

One click creates the fan page as a draft on staging: teaser, how-to, download, Team Card, share buttons.

Week after

Measure

Downloads, logins, Team Cards unlocked, QR scans (UTM print_qr) and #ARF1 posts.

Prototype ready: the Chinese Grand Prix zine and its fan page are on staging as a draft.

Next steps
